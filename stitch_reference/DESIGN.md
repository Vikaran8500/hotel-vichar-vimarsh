## Brand & Style

This design system establishes an editorial, introspective digital sanctuary reflecting contemporary Indian hospitality. The design fuses classical architectural gravitas with high-fashion print editorial standards. The target audience comprises discerning global travelers, design connoisseurs, and cultural patrons seeking mindful luxury and reflective slowness ("Vichar Vimarsh" — contemplation and discourse).

The aesthetic direction is **Refined Editorial Minimalism with Architectural Tactility**:
- **Quiet Confidence:** Generous, unhurried negative space prioritized over dense transactional UI.
- **Print Editorial Sensibility:** Asymmetric compositions, oversized dramatic serif titling, delicate hair-width line work, and strict baseline balance reminiscent of collectible art monographs.
- **Cinematic Atmosphere:** Full-bleed environmental imagery, warm atmospheric depth, and purposeful, fluid micro-interactions moving at an unhurried, graceful cadence.

## Colors

The palette draws directly from carved stone jaalis, oiled brass hardware, hand-loomed raw tussar silk, and twilight shadows across heritage courtyards.

- **Primary (`#1A1917` / Deep Obsidian Charcoal):** Used for authoritative typography, high-contrast framing, primary CTA fills, and deep architectural contrasts. Supported by pure shadow near-black (`#121110`) for immersive full-screen takeovers.
- **Secondary (`#C5A059` / Warm Antique Brass):** The primary metallic accent, evoking gilded temple leafing and artisanal metallurgy. Used strictly for focus states, signature accents, decorative hairline rules, active indicators, and badges.
- **Tertiary (`#9E7D3B` / Deep Burnished Bronze):** Used for hover interactions on gold elements, muted metadata, and secondary decorative highlights requiring higher contrast against ivory surfaces.
- **Neutral Canvas (`#FBF9F5` / Warm Raw Ivory):** The primary ambient background tone, replicating heavy uncoated archival cotton paper. Replaces sterile digital white to create an organic, warm environment.
- **Supporting Neutral Layers:** Champagne beige (`#EFE9DF`) provides surface tinting for elevated cards and modules; muted sandstone (`#D8CFC4`) provides delicate hairline borders, divider strokes, and subtle structural grids.

## Typography

The typographic hierarchy centers on tension between high-contrast transitional serifs and pristine geometric grotesque sans-serifs.

- **Headlines (`Playfair Display`):** Reserved for atmospheric declarations, suite names, curation stories, and spatial prompts. Headlines are set with close line spacing and optical kerning to mimic luxury broadsheet layouts.
- **Body (`Plus Jakarta Sans`):** Clean, open apertures provide effortless legibility against ivory backdrops. Body text balances airy line heights with refined font weights (`300` and `400`).
- **Editorial Sub-labels (`label-caps`):** Set strictly in uppercase with ultra-wide tracking (`+0.22em`). Used above headlines as section counters, category signifiers, and curated amenity indicators.

## Layout & Spacing

The layout language is rooted in an asymmetrical 12-column editorial grid on desktop, transitioning to an 8-column layout on tablet and a single-column rhythm on mobile.

- **Asymmetry & White Space:** Staggered visual anchoring is preferred over modular grid repetition. Imagery often overlaps column boundaries or shifts off-axis across vertical planes, counterbalanced by generous text blocks.
- **Desktop Breakpoint (≥ 1280px):** 12 columns with 64px (`margin`) page margins and 32px (`gutter`) inter-column spaces. High-impact vignettes can bypass margin constraints for full-bleed viewport presentation.
- **Tablet Breakpoint (768px – 1279px):** 8 columns, 32px margins, 24px gutters. Secondary asymmetric offsets collapse into structured balance.
- **Mobile Breakpoint (< 768px):** 4-column flow with 24px (`margin-mobile`) exterior padding and 16px gutters. Editorial scale is maintained by running hero serifs large while keeping metadata light and structured.

## Elevation & Depth

This system avoids floating volumetric drop shadows, plastic blurs, and exaggerated 3D layering. Depth is expressed via **tactile tonal stratification, low-contrast architectural outlines, and controlled light diffusion**:

- **Tonal Planes:** Surface depth relies on shifting paper layers. The foundational floor sits at `#FBF9F5` (Warm Ivory); elevated cards and hovering reservation palettes step to `#EFE9DF` (Champagne Beige); darkened experiential sections drop into `#1A1917` (Deep Obsidian).
- **Hairline Framing:** Component borders utilize a crisp 1px rule in `#D8CFC4` (Muted Sandstone) or `#C5A059` at 30% opacity. Structural boundaries mirror dry-laid stone masonry and brass inlays.
- **Ambient Shadow (Reserved for Floating Modals):** When an element physically hovers (e.g., the booking engine bar or room tour overlay), it casts a diffused, warm-tinted shadow: `box-shadow: 0 24px 48px -12px rgba(26, 25, 23, 0.08)`. This mimics warm sunlight falling softly through a jharokha lattice.

## Shapes

The design system employs **Level 0 (Sharp)** geometry. 

All interactive containers, imagery viewports, form fields, and floating sheets feature unrounded, precise 90-degree corners (`border-radius: 0px`). This architectural precision recalls traditional Indian stone carving, hand-cut paper folios, and structured haveli courtyards. Radii are forbidden across structural components, reserving roundness solely for native circular iconography (such as compasses or subtle astrological glyphs).

## Components

### Buttons
- **Primary Action:** Solid `#1A1917` background with `#FBF9F5` typography, 0px border radius, uppercase `label-caps` styling, and generous padding (`16px 36px`). On hover, transitions smoothly to `#C5A059` text and subtle interior border shift over 400ms.
- **Secondary (Editorial Ghost):** Transparent fill with a 1px solid `#1A1917` or `#C5A059` hairline border, accompanied by an extended micro-arrow glyph (`→`) that translates 4px horizontally upon hover.
- **Tertiary (Text Link):** Simple text set in `label-caps` underlined by an offset hairline rule that expands from 0% to 100% width on hover.

### Form Inputs & Date Pickers
- **Text & Reservation Inputs:** Minimalist underline-only structure (1px `#D8CFC4` bottom border), adopting an active brass highlight (`#C5A059`) on focus. Floating labels shift upward into `label-caps` at 10px. Zero background fills ensure seamless integration into ivory backdrops.
- **Dropdowns & Selectors:** Clean typography with bespoke, hairline brass down-carats.

### Cards & Media Panels
- **Accommodations & Dining Cards:** Asymmetric vertical ratios (4:5 or 3:4 portrait) with sharp framing. Imagery features subtle scale transitions (1.0 to 1.03) within fixed viewports upon pointer hover. Text metadata is positioned below or partially overlapping the image in high-opacity `#FBF9F5` blocks.

### Chips & Badges
- **Curated Tags (e.g., "Courtyard View", "Ayurvedic Spa"):** Rectangular, unrounded enclosures with 1px border in `#D8CFC4`, uppercase `10px` typography, `6px 12px` padding, and transparent backdrops.

### Lists & Dividers
- **Editorial Fact Sheets:** Two-column key-value pairs split by ultra-thin hairline dividers (`1px solid #EFE9DF`). Left column features `label-caps` in `#9E7D3B`; right column features `body-md` in `#1A1917`.

### Checkboxes & Radios
- **Selection Controls:** Custom square forms (0px radius) with 1px `#D8CFC4` frames. Checked state fills with `#1A1917` containing a brass inner hairline diamond or dot.

### Specialized Hospitality Components
- **Floating Concierge / Booking Bar:** Persistent, ultra-slim bottom bar in `#1A1917` with `#FBF9F5` and `#C5A059` accents, providing instantaneous access to reservation calendars and suite availability without visual clutter.
